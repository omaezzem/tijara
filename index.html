    Ga3 l'qawa3id
    • L'hadaf: tb9a akhir wa7ed 3ndo flouss. Kol la3eb kaybda b 15 000 DH.
    • Nobtek: lo7 jouj dés o tmchi. Kol mra katdouz mn "Bda" katakhod 2 000 DH.
    • Double: 3awd lo7. 3 doubles mtab3in f nfs n-nouba = l7abs nichan.
    • Chri: wqefti f mdina, gare wla Lma/Dow bla mol? Tqder techriha b thamanha.
    • Mazad: ila ma bghitich techriha, katdkhol l mazad: ga3 drari (7tta nta) kayzaydo, o li 3ta a3la thaman kayakhodha.
    • L'kra: wqefti f mlk dyal chi wa7ed khor? Khlles lih l'kra. Ila 3ndo ga3 l'mdon dyal nfs l'loun bla dyour, l'kra katdobbel.
    • Gares: 250 / 500 / 1 000 / 2 000 DH 3la 7sab ch7al mn gare 3nd mol l'mlk.
    • Lma o Dow: dés × 40, wla dés × 100 ila 3ndo bjouj.
    • Dyour o hotel: mlli tkoun 3ndek ga3 l'loun (o ta wa7da ma mrhouna), tqder tbni f nobtek. Khassek tbni b l'3adl: ma tzidch dar tanya f wa7da 9bel ma tbni f ga3 l'khrin. 4 dyour → hotel. L'bank fiha ghir 32 dar o 12 hotel.
    • Bi3 dyour: katrdhom l'bank b ness thaman.
    • Rhen: tqder trhen mlk bla dyour o takhod ness thamano. Mlk mrhoun ma kaydkhelch kra. Bach tfekko, khlles ness thaman + 10%.
    • Tbadel: f nobtek tqder te3red 3la chi la3eb tbadel l'mdon o l'flouss. Mdina fiha dyour (wla f loun dyalha) ma tqderch tbadelha.
    • L7abs: katmchi ila wqefti f "Sir l7abs", jbedti karta, wla 3 doubles. Bach tkhrej: dir double, khlles 500 DH 9bel ma tlo7, wla sta3mel karta "Khrjti mn l7abs". Mn ba3d 3 dl mrrat bla double, katkhlles 500 DH o tkhrej. F l7abs mazal katchedd l'kra.
    • Zhar o Sndou9: khod karta o dir chno galt.
    • Dariba: khlles l'montant l'bank. Parking: ma kayn walou.
    • Faillite: ila ma qdertich tkhlles, l'game katbi3 dyourek o terhen mlkek automatiquement. Ila mazal ma kfatch, katkhrej, o mlkek kaymchi l li kan 3ndek lih (wla l'bank).
